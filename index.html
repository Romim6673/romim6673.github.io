THE HISTORY

░ Chronicles of the Damned ░

🏚

The Construction

In 1847, the Blackwood family built their mansion on ground where an ancient burial site lay. The builders heard screams from beneath the soil. Three months later, every worker was dead.

II
🩸

The Disappearance

On October 31st, 1923, every member of the Blackwood family vanished without a trace. The mansion remained untouched for decades. Police who entered came out... different. If they came out at all.

III

The Awakening

Paranormal investigators describe a presence beyond classification. Cold spots that move. Whispers with no source. Shadows that breathe. The walls themselves seem to remember the screams of the past.
